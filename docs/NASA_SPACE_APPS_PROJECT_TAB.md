# Robustness Evaluation of South-Polar Observation and Lunar Visibility Estimates (RESOLVE)

**Short descriptor:** A decision-convergence auditor for lunar landing-window models

## Project description

Robustness Evaluation of South-Polar Observation and Lunar Visibility Estimates (RESOLVE) is a NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026 project for the Lunar Landing Site Navigator challenge. It audits whether modeled lunar Sun/Earth visibility results remain stable across defensible modeling choices.

At dGR101, a common absolute observer gives 7.40 complete simultaneous days with NASA Product 78 SL2 5 m and 12.92 days with native Product 90 80 m, a +5.53-day effect from unrounded results. RESOLVE traces that response to horizon evidence while retaining negative findings, observer and reporting definitions, source provenance and limitations.
