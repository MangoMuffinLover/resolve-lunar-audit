# Robustness Evaluation of South-Polar Observation and Lunar Visibility Estimates (RESOLVE)

guided demonstration scripts

Use the published `/demo/` route at https://resolve-lunar-audit.vercel.app/demo/. It has six manual steps and no automatic playback. The schedules below are rehearsal targets; no human timing or comprehension result has been measured. Rehearse aloud with the actual clicks before recording. This is a demonstration narrative, not an assertion about submission-format rules.

## Ninety-second version

| Target time | View and action | Spoken narration |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–10 s | Step 1: Result | “This lunar site has a modeled 7.40-day window with the Sun and Earth visible together. Before relying on that precision, does the answer survive the model?” |

| 25–45 s | Step 3: Why; show paired skylines | “At one common time, the Sun’s position is identical. The represented skyline differs, changing the visibility decision. The full explanation traces sampled terrain, both bracketing rays, and each model’s own event times.” |
| 45–62 s | Step 4: Assumptions; change observer to 2 m above ground | “Observer convention matters too. The separate Product 104 prediction changes from 2.99 to 7.27 days. Reporting cutoffs also change duration statistics without causing a physical sunset.” |

The deck includes scientific qualifications throughout. Keep them visible rather than cropping them out of the recording. Do not introduce a mission-failure threshold claim from the clipped nominal duration.

## Recording and rehearsal checklist

1. Start at the local `/demo/` route and use **Restart demonstration**. A normal page refresh also resets to step 1.
2. Rehearse the six steps once on the actual machine. Confirm the paired geometry and dGR203 pilot load without error. A missing-data message is a recording stop, not a cue to narrate a chart that is absent.
3. Keep the browser at a readable desktop size. Use the stored 17 November 2026, 14:30 UTC comparison as labeled; do not describe it as each model’s onset.
4. For the two-minute version, expand the own-onset details. Name the distances as sampled lower-ray witnesses, not direct measurements or points on one shared cross-section.
5. Preserve the observer labels and study domains. Keep native NASA grids, the separate SDEM and synthetic averaging distinct.
6. If navigating into another product route during rehearsal, return to `/demo/` and select the intended step. There is no automatic session recording, saved progress or timed advance.
7. Record actual narration duration and any missed action. Adjust pacing after rehearsal; the proposed timestamps are not measured acceptance evidence.

Do not expose a participant unfamiliar with RESOLVE to this deck or script before the uncoached twenty-second flagship test. A successful coached presentation and independent product comprehension answer different questions.
